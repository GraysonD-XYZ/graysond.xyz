This exhibit starts with a simple feedback system where the rule is known, the inputs are visible, and the starting conditions appear controlled. Increase feedback, add a microscopic perturbation, or introduce a tiny noise floor and watch two forecasts split apart. The point is practical: a system can be explainable and still become hard to predict.
